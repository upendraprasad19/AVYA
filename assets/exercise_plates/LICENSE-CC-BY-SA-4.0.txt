ADAPTATION NOTICE (ICANBEFITTER)

The exercise plate drawings in this app are adapted from the above work.
Changes made: each drawing's viewBox was cropped to its ink bounds -- the
union of the start and end frames for a two-position movement, the frame's own
bounds for a static hold -- and the fill was changed from #fff to currentColor
so the app can tint it. No path data was altered.
