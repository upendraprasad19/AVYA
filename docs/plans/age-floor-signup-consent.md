## 1. Evidence (writers and readers)

**Age.** Three date-of-birth pickers carried three floors: onboarding identity step `lib/features/onboarding/screens/identity_screen.dart:82` (10; the U10 hotfix after a tester entered his son's birth year), the conversational onboarding step `onboarding_chat_screen.dart:500` (16, `365 * 16` days), Edit Profile `edit_profile_screen.dart:677` (13). Writers of `date_of_birth`: `identity_screen.dart` (`'date_of_birth': _dob!.toIso8601String()`, line ~483), the chat step's `setAnswer` (`onboarding_chat_screen.dart:~503`), Edit Profile; readers: BMR / nutrition (`nutrition_provider.dart:86`, `nutrition_repository.dart:556`), `onboarding_provider.dart:386`, `user_repository.dart:1114`. No reader enforces an age; the pickers are the only gate. `git grep -n "showDatePicker(\|ScrollDatePicker("` lists exactly these three plus the widget definition.

**Consent.** Writer: `sign_in_screen.dart:1096-1097` passes `termsAcceptedAt: now (UTC)` and `termsVersion: AppConstants.termsVersion` to `signUpWithEmail`; `auth_provider.dart:1293-1294` writes them to Hive `userBox` after the session opens and the cloud row follows through `_ensureLocalUser`. Google and phone sign-ups have no checkbox: `AuthSessionBootstrapper.ensureTermsConsentFallback` (`auth_session_bootstrapper.dart`) stamps `terms_accepted_at = users.created_at` and `terms_version = AppConstants.termsVersion` when no stamp exists, and the Welcome screen footer already reads "By continuing, you agree to our Privacy Policy and Terms" (`welcome_screen.dart`). Readers of the stamp: `auth_session_bootstrapper.dart:683-719,802-808,918-931` (hydration); nothing compares `terms_version` (the `TermsModal` its doc comment named no longer exists).

**Round-1 corrections (v2).** (1) The conversational picker's `ScrollDatePicker` bounded only `lastDate.year`: a month or day later in that year was reachable (born 31 December 2013 passes a 2013 ceiling). (2) `identity_screen.dart` CONTINUE checked only `_dob != null`, so a date of birth carried in through the route extras (an earlier session, or the old floor of 10) skipped the floor. (3) `integration_test/flows/auth_flow_test.dart` T3 and `integration_test/helpers/auth_helper.dart` still drove the removed checkbox. (4) `docs/operations/GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` rows 3.1, 3.4, 3.5 described the checkbox. (5) With email confirmation ON (the live setting) the sign-up has no session, so `_ensureLocalUser` cannot write the stamp from the tap: the stamp is written by `ensureTermsConsentFallback` after the confirm link, at `created_at`, labelled by `termsVersionForAccountCreatedAt`; the tap's own time is stamped only when the session exists at once. Both give a timestamp at or within minutes of the sign-up, which is what the audit needs. (6) Re-opening a picker with a saved date of birth 10 to 13 years old does not crash (both date-picker screens clamp `initialDate`), but pressing OK without moving it stores today minus 13 years; Cancel keeps the old value. Accepted: it is the "left alone" decision's visible edge. (7) `kMinAgeYears` is measured on the device clock (local time), not IST; a traveller can differ by a day. (8) `termsV2EffectiveFromUtc` is the IST midnight of 16 October (`2026-10-15T18:30:00Z`), a date boundary and not the publish instant.

## 2. Changes

1. `lib/core/utils/age_floor.dart`: `kMinAgeYears = 13` and `latestAllowedDob(now)` (today minus 13 years; 29 February clamps to 28 February, not 1 March). The three pickers call it; none keeps a literal. `ScrollDatePicker` clamps to `lastDate` (month and day, not only the year) and `IdentityScreen` CONTINUE refuses a carried-in date of birth later than the ceiling ("You must be 13 or older to use AVYA Fit. Pick your date of birth again.").
2. `sign_in_screen.dart`: the unchecked checkbox row and `_privacyAccepted` are removed; `_ConsentNotice` ("By tapping CREATE ACCOUNT, you agree to our Privacy Policy and Terms.", both links) sits above the button; the button is enabled by the form validation alone; the tap still stamps the UTC time and version. Comments record the decision and the stated risk.
3. `AppConstants.termsVersion` `'v1'` -> `'v2'`, plus `termsV2EffectiveFromUtc = '2026-10-15T18:30:00Z'` (IST midnight of 16 October); the stale doc comment is rewritten (nothing compares the version; a bump labels new acceptances only).
4. `AuthSessionBootstrapper.termsVersionForAccountCreatedAt`: the Google / phone fallback stamp records `v1` for an account created before the effective instant (it never saw the v2 text) and the current version otherwise, instead of labelling every legacy account `v2`.
5. OI-346 (minted): existing accounts accepted v1 or nothing, and no flow asks them about v2.
6. The notice uses `textDim`, not the Welcome footer's `textMute` (about 3.5:1, below AA): it is the consent mechanism. The device tests (`integration_test/flows/auth_flow_test.dart` T3, `helpers/auth_helper.dart`) lose the checkbox; the vacuous `TermsModal` gate group of `test/auth/terms_skip_test.dart` (a test-local copy of a widget that no longer exists) is removed.
7. Docs: OI-305 and OI-310 progress lines (written on top of PR #117's branch, which holds the decision text), `lib/features/auth/CLAUDE.md`, `docs/architecture/auth-detail.md`, `functionality-flow.md`, `sot_registry.yaml` (terms acceptance), `naming_conventions.md` (the new symbols), `docs/operations/GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md`. `lib/features/onboarding/CLAUDE.md` already said "min age 13" (it was wrong until now).

## 4. Risk, stated

- **Consent reading (founder's call).** "By continuing" is the weaker DPDP reading; the Welcome footer and the OAuth fallback already use it. If counsel or Play review objects, restoring the checkbox is a local change in `sign_in_screen.dart` and the tests above flip with it.
- **13 to 17.** A floor of 13 does not meet DPDP's parental-consent rule for under-18s; the policy tells under-18s to use the app with a parent's knowledge and says it does not track their behaviour or show ads. OI-305 stays open with that caveat. Whether the app honours the policy's under-18 statements (no behavioural tracking, template-only progress review) is outside this batch.
- **Existing under-13 profiles** (none found on 2026-10-04: youngest 24) are left alone; the policy says such an account is deleted when learned of.
- **Existing accounts and the new policy**: OI-346.
