### Task 3: Keyboard fold (C21)

**Why not `MediaQuery`:** `Scaffold` (default `resizeToAvoidBottomInset: true`, `active_workout/screen.dart:461`) strips the bottom view inset from its body (`flutter/lib/src/material/scaffold.dart:2923-2924`), so `MediaQuery.viewInsetsOf(context).bottom` is always 0 inside a card and a fold keyed to it NEVER fires on a phone. The fold reads the view: a small `State` mixing `WidgetsBindingObserver`, `didChangeMetrics` -> `setState`, `View.of(context).viewInsets.bottom > 0` (physical pixels; only the sign matters), on the focused card only, independent of `_logMode`.

- Test with the REAL `Scaffold` above the card (the harness provides one, `card_harness.dart:107-118`) and `tester.view.viewInsets = const FakeViewPadding(bottom: 300)` (then `tester.pump()`; reset in `addTearDown`; verified valid: `flutter_test/lib/src/window.dart:1184-1190` fires the metrics callback): the hero shows `folded: true` (the 44 px thumb in its place); with `viewInsets.bottom == 0` it is unfolded; runs with log mode on and off. A case that wraps `MediaQuery(viewInsets: ...)` BELOW the Scaffold is the blind one and is NOT the test. Mutations: read `MediaQuery.viewInsetsOf(context)` (the Scaffold test must FAIL, proving it is not blind); tie the fold to `_logMode`.
- Device check owed (not a unit test): the tapped set field stays visible and under the finger; if it jumps, the fold shrinks the tile instead of removing it. `blocked_on_user` until a device pass.
