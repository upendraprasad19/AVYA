# Plan: Quads reach their weekly floor in the 6-day build_muscle split (OI-454, SBF-QUADS-FLOOR option a)

Date 2026-11-06. Branch `oi454-quads`. Tier L / `platform` (plan_engine). Execution mode: INLINE (one worktree, one writer), decided at start.
Founder direction 2026-11-03 (OI-454): swap Legs B's glute-isolation slot for a Quads knee_dominant compound slot AND add an allocator co-group policy, in one batch. Rule-14 waiver for `plan_generator.dart` given in chat ("ok"); the only edit there is one argument of the existing `SessionBudgetContext(...)` call. ## Problem (measured on the unchanged tree, week 1, real `generateV4`)
Intermediate build_muscle, full_gym, 6 days, 90 minutes: Quads 8 weekly sets against a band of 12 to 16 (floor 12). Legs A holds Barbell Back Squat 3, Deadlift 3, Hanging Leg Raise 5, Leg Extension 5, Glute Bridge 2, Standing Calf Raise 5; Legs B holds no quad exercise. Glutes sit at 16 = their top, and `_label` reports Hamstrings `co_group_limited`. The allocator refuses a set when ANY group the exercise credits is at its weekly top or session cap (`BudgetLedger.fits`, `session_budget.dart:568`), and a squat, lunge or leg press lists `['Quads', 'Glutes']`, so Glutes (filled by Deadlift, Romanian Deadlift and Glute Bridge) blocks the quad work. The spike on 2026-11-03 showed a Legs B quad slot alone changes nothing (the allocator drops it).

Quads below the floor, 6-day build_muscle, 90 minutes, before: beginner 2 (bodyweight, dumbbells, basic gym) / 6 (full gym); intermediate 5 / 5 / 3 / 8; advanced 5 / 5 / 3 / 8 (bodyweight / dumbbells / basic gym / full gym).

- Only `build_muscle` + 6 days is affected. The default 6-day PPL, 3/4/5-day splits, every other goal, and the legacy `DaySlot` split are not.

## Decisions
D1. **Slot.** In Legs B replace `Glutes / hip_isolation / isolation / priority 2` with `Quads / knee_dominant / compound / priority 2`. Priority 2 keeps it after the hamstring and glute compounds (priority 1), so a short day drops it before them, and a deload (priority 1 only) is unchanged. A swap, not an add: Legs B stays at 8 slots. Glute work on Legs B is still carried by Glutes / hip_dominant compound (priority 1), Deadlift and Romanian Deadlift; Legs A keeps its Glutes / hip_isolation slot.
D2. **Co-group policy.** New `SessionBudgetContext.quadPrimaryCreditsGlutes` (default `true` = today's credit everywhere). When `false`, `BudgetMuscleGroups.creditedOf(primary_muscles, quadPrimaryCreditsGlutes: false)` drops Glutes from an exercise whose FIRST listed muscle is Quads (77 library rows list Quads first with Glutes; an exercise that also credits Hamstrings, Trap Bar Deadlift, keeps its Glutes credit): the exercise credits Quads (and any other group it lists), so the Glutes top and Glutes session cap never refuse it. It is the founder's "the cap applied to the primary muscle", expressed as the exercise's credit set so that the allocator, week 2, titration and the gate oracle all agree by construction (one function, one flag, no per-call-site branch). `generateV4` sets it `false` iff `planGoal == 'build_muscle' && daysPerWeek == 6`.
D3. **Why scoped, not global.** A global rule was prototyped and measured: it changes 745 of 999 generator-golden keys (every plan with a squat or lunge, in every goal and day count), far past the OI's "golden changes only for 6-day build_muscle personas". Scoped, 60 keys change, all `grid|*|build_muscle|6d|*`. The other day counts have their own Quads shortfalls (measured in Task 1, residue below) and are filed as a separate OI rather than changed here.
D4. **Acceptance (measured BEFORE finalising, Task 1):** in the founder case Quads >= 12 in week 1, no group above its band top or session cap, every day inside its minutes, Glutes and Hamstrings not below their floors because of it. Other 6-day build_muscle personas: Quads never LOWER than before and above the old figure in all 24 cells (3 levels x 4 tiers x 60 / 90 minutes). The OI's wording "the other 6-day personas reach the floor" is only partly met (5 of 12 are short at 90 minutes, 9 of 12 at 60); the short ones are named with their allocator label and filed as OI-477, not hidden.

## Risks and mirror cases
- **Week 2 / titration disagree with the allocator.** All three build their credit sets from the one flag via `creditedOf`; Task 3 asserts that no group is over its credited top or session cap in weeks 1 to 4 of phases 1 to 5 (the week-2 pass is exercised by every sweep week; `VolumeTitration` does not run on a real plan, `enable_volume_titration` is default OFF, so the consumers test is its proof).
- **Glutes under-counted.** A squat's glute work no longer counts toward the Glutes band in this split, so Glutes can end below where full credit put it. Measured: founder case Glutes 13 (floor 12), still in band; advanced personas stay at 20 (top). Beginner 90 minutes: Glutes 12 (floor 10). Stated, not hidden.
- **The same squat on both legs days.** The Legs B quad slot can resolve to the same exercise as Legs A (Barbell Back Squat on both, 5 + 4 sets). That is frequency, as for Push A/B; the slot is not deduplicated across days.
- **Lighter equipment / injuries.** The slot resolves through the same cascade, capability and injury filters as every other knee_dominant slot; a knee-injured user gets the same substitutes. The injury sweeps and `kThinCeilings` are the proof; their counts may move and are re-frozen in the same commit.
- **A day that was 8 slots stays 8** (swap). No fixed "slots per day" invariant moves.
- **Installed-copy / old data.** Plans already generated on a tester phone do not change until they regenerate or advance a phase (no migration; testers only).
