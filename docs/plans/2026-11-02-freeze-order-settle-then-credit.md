# Plan: settle-then-credit order for the weekly streak-freeze refill (D1)

Branch `freeze-order-d1`. Execution mode: **inline** (one coordinator, one worktree; decided at start, not switched). Tier: **L / platform** (streak freeze state is synced; `lib/core/services/**`). Closes ledger row `FRS-D1-ORDER` of `docs/audit/freeze-refill-sync.closure.yaml`.

## 1. The founder's rule (confirmed, verbatim intent)

A freeze is a KEPT stock. Days up to Saturday are settled FIRST: a missed scheduled day spends a freeze, a Sunday rest is free. THEN, when the app is opened on Monday, +1 is credited (cap 1 free / 3 PRO), regardless of last week's misses. A PRO user at 3 freezes who misses 6 days keeps the streak 3 days and loses it on the 4th. The next Monday's +1 covers a missed Monday (settled Tuesday).

Consequence: with stock 0 and a missed Saturday, the streak BREAKS on Saturday (nothing to spend) and the Monday +1 arrives afterwards as 1. Today the rollover path credits first, so that +1 is silently spent on Saturday and the streak survives.

## 3. Design (r7, after plan-review rounds 1-6; SIMPLIFIED: the separate pre-pass and the caller reorder are removed)

Rounds 1 (3 P1), 2 (1 P0, 3 P1) and 3 (1 P1) each found a real gap in a growing mechanism. Round 3 showed the pre-pass lived in the wrong place: ANY reckon that runs before the week's credit lands (completeWorkout, the PRO ensure, `reckonAndNotifyAfterRestore`, a rollover that is reordered) is an unbounded walk and spends old stock on Monday-onward misses. So the rule moves INTO the walk, and the separate `settleFreezesBefore` pre-pass and the `_doRolloverWithRef` reorder are deleted (fewer moving parts, per the batch's own history).

**3.1 Two rules inside `_calculateStreak`** (read-only display and consuming reckon share them, so every surface agrees). Let `thisMonday` = IST Monday of `nowWall()`, `stamp` = `streak_freezes_last_refill`.
- **Cutoff (settle before credit).** While the weekly credit is DUE (`stamp != null && stamp < thisMonday`; a NULL stamp = never credited = no cutoff: DECISION (round-5 item 2, written down, not implicit): a null stamp (a legacy or clamp-migrated account, `StreakFreezeClampMigrator` removes the stamp once) keeps the OLD order for its FIRST credit only: reckon #1 has no cutoff, so on a Tuesday/Wednesday open it may spend old stock on Monday and break at Saturday, then the credit stamps this Monday. One-time, testers only; pinned by a Tuesday-clock test with a null stamp and a mutant that treats null as due; also keeps the many streak tests that seed no stamp weekday-independent), a fresh missed day with `date >= thisMonday` is skipped (`continue`: not spent, not chain-breaking). So before the credit only days before this Monday are settled, with the OLD stock; Monday-onward misses wait for the credit. Founder: "next Monday's +1 covers a missed Monday".
- **Horizon (a break is durable; round-2 P0).** A fresh missed day (not completed, not today, not in `usedDates`) with `date < stamp` is unfundable: `break`. Anything before the credited Monday was settled before the credit and could not be paid then; later stock (the next credit) cannot resurrect it. Applies only when `streakReckonUserGateEnabled` (legacy switch off) AND `stamp <= thisMonday` (a future-dated stamp from a wrong clock / year-sim must not make every miss unfundable). `stamp == null` -> no horizon.
Both are OFF under the kill switch (3.5), so OFF is the pre-batch walk exactly.

**3.1b Walk hygiene (round-4 P2).** The flag is read ONCE before the loop and `thisMonday` / the parsed stamp are computed only when it is ON (the OFF walk is byte-for-byte the old one, no `configBox` read in the 365-iteration loop). `thisMonday` is the SAME key `refillIfNewWeek` uses: one shared helper `istMondayKey(DateTime)` used by both. Round-5 P1: it MUST NOT pass `mondayOfIst`'s output through `formatDateKey`/`istDateStr` (that converts the instant a second time and is one day early east of IST); it formats the components directly, exactly as `refillIfNewWeek` does today (`m.year/m.month/m.day`, zero-padded), so the kill-switch-OFF refill body is unchanged. Pinned by a unit test on a hand-built naive `DateTime` (a `TZ` is fixed per test process, CI is IST) plus a source-grep that the helper never feeds `mondayOfIst` into `formatDateKey`/`istDateStr`; the file is also run once under `TZ=Asia/Tokyo`. Gate 19 (`check_hive_map_field_drift.dart`): the walk reads the stamp through a typed accessor `UserRepository.getFreezeLastRefill()` (like `getPhaseStartedAtIso`), not a literal map read in `workout_repository.dart`. Both rules are inactive while `SyncService.pausedForSimulation` is true, so the dev year-sim keeps the old walk (its reckons are gate-closed by the unset marker and its direct `consumeMissedDayIfFreezeAvailable` would otherwise meet the horizon on every weekend).
**3.1c Refill step order.** Step (3) wraps reckon #1 in try/catch with telemetry (`streak_freeze_refill_settle_failed`); decision, written down: the credit STILL lands (the credit never depended on the walk and a throwing walk must not silently cost a week's +1), reckon #2 then runs normally.

**3.2 `refillIfNewWeek()`** (after `willRefill` is true): (1) kill switch -> verbatim old behaviour; (2) deferral: return null (no write) + LOW event `streak_freeze_refill_deferred` iff `SyncFlags.streakReckonUserGateEnabled && !SyncService.restoreSettledForCurrentUser && !SyncService.pausedForSimulation` (the real simulation seam, `sync_service.dart:159`); (3) otherwise, UNLESS the legacy switch `disable_streak_reckon_user_gate` is ON (then skip reckon #1: verbatim old body, because both rules are off in that mode and an unbounded reckon before the credit would be the old wrong order, round-5 P2-4), `WorkoutRepository.instance.reckonStreakDecayAndPersist()` (the credit is due, so by 3.1 it settles ONLY days before this Monday with the old stock; gated like today: no schedule row = nothing to settle), then `commitRefill`, then `reckonStreakDecayAndPersist()` again (stamp is now `thisMonday`: it spends Monday-onward misses against the new stock and persists the streak count). `_doRolloverWithRef` is NOT reordered: the refill stays first and now does its own settle.

**3.3 Deferral rationale and accepted harm.** No schedule-row exception: a reinstall / new device has an empty `workoutBox` yet real cloud history; a brand-new account's restore of an empty cloud also settles the marker (`shouldSettleRestoreMarker`, `sync_service.dart:2013`; `restoring_screen.dart:116/378` run on every cold start and sign-in), so a new user is credited right after it (heal `heal_after_restore.dart:52` / `restoring_screen.dart:551`). Under the legacy switch `disable_streak_reckon_user_gate` the tick is the reckon's gate, the deferral is OFF and the horizon is OFF (so a credit landing while the reckon is closed cannot strand unsettled misses). `_restoreSettledUserId` is process memory: offline cold start, the CONTINUE escape, or a streak-critical op failing every launch leave it unset. Today those users still get the credit and only the decay is off; with the deferral they get none that week, and weeks do not accumulate, so an unsettled week's +1 is lost for good. Accepted: the app is not live (testers only), the decay is equally off for them, and a bounded fallback would reopen the credit-before-settle path. Written into the diagnose-doc and the ledger.

**3.4 No retry in `reckonAndNotifyAfterRestore`** (redundant: the marker is set before the heal's refill; a withheld marker defers it and every later resume rollover retries).

**3.5 Kill switch** `disable_settle_before_refill` (`SyncFlags.settleBeforeRefillEnabled`, opt-out, box-not-open -> ON, try/catch like `freezePushAttemptsEnabled`): OFF removes cutoff, horizon and deferral.

**3.6 Accepted consequences, stated in the diagnose-doc.** (a) A mixed-version pair (older build credits first and spends on Saturday, newer credits 1, same stamp): the same-week LOWER merge takes 0 and the newer device's credit is lost (not a regression, testers). (b) Cross-device restore: if device A's credit push landed but a consume push was dropped (rare with the 3-attempt retry), device B holds a stamp newer than the unspent misses and the horizon turns what was a double charge into a chain break. (c) The fresh credit can be spent on a Monday miss while the streak is already broken behind it (stock is spent on missed days; not a regression). (b2) The same class through the push-conflict retry merge (`sync_restore_completeness.dart:266-289`, `:429-450`) and `_restoreFreezes`, which can adopt a newer cloud stamp over unspent local misses (cloudWins). (d) Legacy testers whose old unspent misses precede the stamped Monday lose that old chain at the next walk. (f) The horizon unfunds a pre-stamp miss whose row appears AFTER the stamp lands (PRO phase ensure `pro_phase_advance.dart:748`, a late restore of schedule rows, an un-pause), and stock changed after the stamp (`grantFirstProFreezes`, a mid-week upgrade, `resetToFreeCapOnLapse`) cannot fund pre-stamp days: accepted, testers only, documented by a test. (g) While the credit is due but cannot land (restore unsettled / offline / CONTINUE) the cutoff hides this week's misses in the display and the reckon is gated too, so the Home chain is optimistic for up to a week; once a credit of 1 arrives with two misses (Mon, Tue, stock 0) the newest-first walk funds Tue and breaks at Mon, so the display drops when the credit lands. Both follow the founder rule. (e) One Monday open can now produce two freeze writes (consume, then refill) and up to two `syncFreezes` pushes; the notice reads "N freezes used" and the chip shows the post-credit stock.

Not changed: `grantFirstProFreezes` (OI-448), the push retry, `mergeFreezeProgress`, the chip providers, any Edge Function (verified: no server twin of the walk; `rank_engine.ts` reads the persisted count). No migration, no schema. CQRS gate: `streakSettleOpen`-style helpers stay pure getters that call no `consume*`/`reckon*`; names added: `disable_settle_before_refill`, `streak_freeze_refill_deferred`.

## 4. Mirror cases to test

- stock 0, missed Saturday, Monday open, restore settled: spend nothing (0), streak breaks at Saturday, THEN +1 -> stock 1, `last_refill` = Monday. (Old: stock 1 spent on Saturday -> 0, streak kept.)
- stock 2, missed Saturday: spend one (1), then +1 -> 2.
- PRO stock 3, 6 missed days: spends 3, streak keeps 3 days, breaks on the 4th; then +1.
- Sunday rest: no spend.
- Monday itself missed: not penalised on Monday (settled Tuesday), covered by next Monday's +1.
- first open of the week on a TUESDAY / WEDNESDAY (Monday missed, Saturday missed, stock 1): Saturday settled first (stock 0), credit +1 (1), Monday spends it (0); streak intact. Same with stock 0: Saturday breaks the chain, credit 1, Monday spends it.
- cold start, restore NOT settled, schedule rows present: refill deferred (no write: stock, `last_refill`, ledger unchanged; event logged); after `reckonAndNotifyAfterRestore`: settle, then credit.
- reinstall / new device: EMPTY `workoutBox`, restore not settled: refill deferred (no credit before the restore).
- brand-new account: empty restore settles the marker, then the credit lands via the heal path.
- legacy switch `disable_streak_reckon_user_gate` ON: no deferral, credit lands (tick 0 -> pre-pass skipped, as the reckon is).
- DURABILITY (round-2 P0): stock 0, Saturday missed, Monday open -> chain broken, stock 1; then a SECOND reckon (resume / midnight / completeWorkout) leaves stock 1 and the broken chain; PRO stock 3, 6 misses: 3 spent, 4th unfunded; the NEXT Monday's credit (+1) does NOT resurrect it (the 4th miss is before the new stamp), the new stock stays 1.
- DIRECT RECKON (round 3 P1): Tuesday open, credit due, stock 1, Saturday and Monday missed, NO refill call: a bare `reckonStreakDecayAndPersist()` / completeWorkout reckon spends the old stock on Saturday only (Monday waits), never on Monday.
- future-dated stamp (> this Monday): no horizon.
- horizon boundary: a miss ON the stamped Monday is still fundable; a miss the day before is not.
- simulation: bypasses the deferral.
- kill switch ON: refill-first order restored byte-for-byte.
- second refill call the same Monday: no-op (idempotent), no second reckon.
