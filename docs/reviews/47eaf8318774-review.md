Effect in production: users whose `app_event` volume alone crosses 50 get selected by the RPC every night, forcing a full paged fetch of their entire conversation history, then skipped at `index.ts:314` once the (correctly) filtered fetch comes up short — a real, unbounded, and growing wasted-cost pattern the fix claims to have closed.
- **verification:** `grep -n "get_users_with_message_count" supabase/functions/rolling-context/index.ts supabase/migrations/010_add_indexes_idempotency_rpc.sql` — shows the RPC is called first (`index.ts:143`) and its SQL body (`010:76-83`) carries no `channel` predicate; `grep -rn "summarized" supabase/migrations/*.sql supabase/functions/*/index.ts` shows the column is set only via `DEFAULT false` and never written `true`.

- **status:** accepted — FIXED. DEFAULT false` at `010:69`; repo-wide grep confirms nothing writes it true).
