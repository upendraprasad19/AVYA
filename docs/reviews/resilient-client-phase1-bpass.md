## Finding 1 — P2 — guard_without_its_mirror (durability of the owed sweep) — VERIFIED
- **file:line:** lib/core/services/sync_service.dart:1030-1033 (reader of `last_full_sync`), :1354 (the only writer), lib/core/services/sync_retry_controller.dart:201-211 (`_afterFailedRun`, state is memory-only); claims in docs/diagnoses/2026-10-01-supabase-api-outage-client-resilience-e5b2a9.md ("HONEST LIMITS (3)") and plan residual (5).
