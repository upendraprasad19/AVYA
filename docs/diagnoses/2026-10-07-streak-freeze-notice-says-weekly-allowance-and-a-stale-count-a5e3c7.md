# a5e3c7 — the freeze notice described a weekly allowance and showed a stale count

## What happened

After a missed scheduled day the app spends a Streak Freeze automatically and Home shows a snackbar. The sentence said "Streak Freeze used! N remaining this week." Three things were wrong with it. It framed freezes as a weekly allowance, which they are not (a kept stock refilled by one every Monday). It never said how many were spent. And N was read from `streak_freeze_remaining_after_use`, a copy of the count taken when the freeze was spent, so a Monday refill or a restore landing before Home showed the notice left the copy stale: one freeze held, "0 remaining" shown. The explainer sheet carried the same model ("per week") and promised the freeze would keep the streak alive, which the kept rule (a freeze is still spent when the streak breaks anyway) does not promise.

## Writer / reader map

Writers: `StreakProgressService.commitConsume` (`streak_progress_service.dart:167`) and its pure count helper `freezeNoticeCountAfterConsume` (`:121`); the one clearer `UserRepository.clearStreakFreezeNotice` (`user_repository.dart:853`). Readers: Home's `_checkStreakFreezeUsed` (`home_screen.dart:225`), which takes the text through `StreakProgressService.takeFreezeNotice` (`streak_progress_service.dart:140`; it reads through `streakFreezeNoticeFromProgress`, `streak_freeze_copy.dart:42`, and clears in the same call); the explainer sheet (`streak_explainer_sheet.dart:92-93`, rules 3 and 4); the cold-start clear (`restoring_screen.dart:519`). The LIVE count the notice shows is `streak_freezes_available`, whose writers are `commitConsume` and `commitRefill`.

## Fix and what it deliberately does not do

See `proposed_fix`. It does not change the rule (founder, ledger F8: keep it), any consume or refill, the freeze restore-merge, or the cloud. It does not remove the snapshot key (other code may still read it; the notice now uses it only as a fallback). It does not try to prove on a device what the snackbar looks like.

## Verification

`test/contracts/streak_freeze_notice_copy_test.dart`: every approved row of the copy table, argument order, edge inputs, the stale-snapshot case in both directions, the accumulation rule as a pure table, and the explainer sheet RENDERED (free and PRO) with the old wording absent and the sibling rules unchanged; plus a PRESENCE-ONLY wiring group. `test/contracts/streak_freeze_notice_behavioral_test.dart`: real Hive, the real writer and clearer: one debit, two debits before the notice is shown, one walk that spends two dates, an old flag with no count, the stale snapshot end to end (spend with none left, Monday refill, notice says "1 left"), the clearer clearing all three keys, being a delta (every other progress field survives), and the next debit restarting at one. Round 1 of the review (below) added: `takeFreezeNotice` handed out once (the second take returns null, no map is minted when there is no progress), a writer-side tolerance test (a string or NaN stored count still lets the freeze debit land), NaN and infinity read as unknown, the PRO note following the caps it is given, a repo-wide scan that no file under lib/ writes the flag false except the one clearer, and the sheet's caps tied to the weekly refill's. See `mutation_proof`.

Review: a fresh context-blind B-pass, two seats (A correctness / writer-reader / copy fidelity, B test adequacy), then a second round over what round 1 changed; findings and dispositions in `docs/reviews/streak-freeze-notice-bpass.md`; ledger entries `U6-BPASS-A`, `U6-BPASS-B`, `U6-BPASS-C`.

## Limits

The SnackBar widget itself is not pumped: `_checkStreakFreezeUsed` is a private method of a ~1,000-line screen. What is behavioural now: the text (copy table), the live count, the accumulation, the read-and-clear-once (`takeFreezeNotice`) and the clearer, all against real Hive. What stays PRESENCE-ONLY: that Home calls `takeFreezeNotice()` and shows `Text(notice, ...)`, and that the restoring screen calls the clearer after its flag read. A device check of the real snackbar and the explainer sheet is owed (CLAUDE.md section 5). The freeze caps (free 1, PRO 3) are stated in five places in `lib/`: the weekly refill (`streak_progress_service.dart:239`), the Home badge and cap provider (`home_provider.dart:304` and `:323`), the Profile service-record tile (`rank_service_record_sheet.dart:211`) and the explainer sheet's own `freeMax`/`proMax`; a presence-only test pins all five to the same numbers, so a one-sided cap change reddens it until the others follow. The Profile tile is changed too (item 9) and is pinned by a rendered test of the real sheet; the only thing not pinned is the on-phone look, which is the same owed device check.
