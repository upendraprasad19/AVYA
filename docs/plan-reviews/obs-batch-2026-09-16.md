## Plan-review round 2 (post-round-1 hardened diff)

`docs/plan-reviews/obs-batch-2026-09-16-round2.md` — explicitly scrutinized

"the corrections themselves can introduce new defects." Found exactly that,

1. **(P3)** Round 1's WardCard-suppression guard, gated on

   leaving the screen top silently blank. Refined the guard to also require
   `weekDays.isEmpty` — the actual condition the phase-lock card below is

   also silently no-ops the pre-existing manual "Retry now" tap whenever it
   races an in-flight auto-drain, with zero indication to the user. Added a
   coalesced-rerun flag (mirroring this codebase's existing `SyncCoalescer`
   "in-flight + dirty do-while" pattern) so an overlapping call is never
   dropped — it guarantees a fresh pass starts right after the in-flight one
   finishes.
3. **(P4, documentation)** A stale test-count denominator ("1 of 3" should be
   "1 of 4") in one diagnose-doc's mutation-proof claim — corrected.
